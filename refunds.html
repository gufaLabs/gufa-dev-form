Refund & Cancellation Policy

gufa.Labs, Ahmedabad. Last updated November 2026.

Developing film can’t be undone, so once your rolls are submitted, we’re unable to offer refunds or cancellations. Payment is taken in full upfront, when you drop your rolls.

  • If a roll comes back blank or black, we’ll refund ₹200 for that roll.
  • In the very rare case that the error is ours, we’ll tell you honestly and replace your film with a free one.
  • Film lost or damaged in transit by a courier is the sender’s risk. Our liability is limited to the remedies above.

Few labs stand behind a roll the way we do. A blank or black roll gets ₹200 back, and if the error was ever ours, a free film.
